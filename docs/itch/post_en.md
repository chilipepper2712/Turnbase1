# Doodle Battlefield (playtest build 0.1)

**Tagline:** Turn-based tactics on a hand-drawn map: humans, orcs and the undead. No dice; every attack is previewed.

## Description

Doodle Battlefield is a turn-based tactics game you play right in the browser, on desktop or phone. Command the human army against an orc horde or an undead host led by a necromancer, on randomly generated maps of forests, hills, rivers, villages and strongholds.

There is no luck involved. Before every attack you see exactly how much damage you will deal, how much you will take back, and whether the target falls. Victory comes from positioning: take the high ground, hold the woods, flank, and keep your troops from breaking.

Each faction plays differently:
- **Humans** draw strength from structures: villages, towers, strongholds. Their marines cut timber and build boats to cross rivers.
- **Orcs** draw strength from the wild: forests and hills. Goblins hide in the woods and spring ambushes.
- **The Undead** know no fear and never flee. Your fallen leave graves, and the whole battlefield is cursed: every grave not laid to rest rises as undead, in just 2 turns near the necromancer. Spend a squad's turn to bury the fallen and deny them. Kill the necromancer and the whole host collapses.

This is a **playtest build**. Feedback is very welcome: what is confusing, what is too easy or too hard, what is broken. Leave a comment below.

## Features

- Turn-based isometric tactics on procedurally generated maps (seeded), 3 map sizes
- 3 factions: you lead the Humans against Orcs or the Undead (necromancer, graves of the fallen, raising enemy dead)
- 4 missions: Skirmish, Hold out, Raze the camp, Escort (bring the general to safety)
- No dice: exact preview of every attack's outcome
- Terrain as a weapon: defense, high ground, range and movement cost differ per faction
- Morale and routing: eager troops hit harder, wavering troops may break and flee
- Fortify, merge and split squads, veterans level up with experience
- Goblin ambushes in the forest, marines building boats
- Two AI difficulty levels, save and load
- Pixel-art terrain or hand-drawn paper style (toggle)
- 3-minute tutorial for newcomers (skippable)
- Mouse or touch controls; English and Vietnamese

## Quick start

- Tap or click a friendly unit to select it, tap a yellow tile to move, tap an enemy to preview the attack, then confirm.
- Tap an empty tile, or press and hold any tile, to inspect the terrain.
- The flag button at the top reopens the mission briefing.

## Notes for 0.1

- Balance has been measured with AI-vs-AI games, not yet with many human players.
- Hold out against the Undead on large maps is still very hard for the Undead; small or medium maps are recommended.
- Best played in landscape.
